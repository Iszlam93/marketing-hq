# Islam's LinkedIn voice guide

Built from Islam's own posts: the MarketMap demo, the desk story, and "Tools are how agents touch the real world". The two Marketing HQ posts were co-written with Claude, so they count as secondary evidence only. Every Brand HQ agent writes to this guide.

## The shape of a post
1. **Hook (1 line):** a confident statement, a contrast, or a time marker. No question openers.
   - "Tools are how agents touch the real world."
   - "Sometimes, success only makes sense when you remember where you started."
   - "For the first time, here's MarketMap in motion."
2. **Context (2 to 4 short lines):** what happened or what I built, told plainly.
3. **The idea:** built line by line, one sentence per paragraph. Often a list with "-" bullets or "→" arrows.
4. **Honest status:** where it really stands. "It's still an early prototype." Never oversell.
5. **The contrast closer:** one or two short sentences that pair opposites.
   - "Tools move the work. Boundaries keep people in control."
   - "The difference is not only intelligence. It is access."
6. **A specific question:** practical and answerable in a few words.
   - "Which view would be more useful in your next planning meeting: the journey or the year?"

## Rules
- **Short paragraphs.** Mostly one sentence, rarely more than two. White space is part of the rhythm.
- **Concrete details carry credibility:** years, objects, numbers, durations. "That small white IKEA desk has been there since around 2017." "In 40 seconds." "Under 20 minutes."
- **Plain words.** No buzzwords, no "game-changer", no "revolutionise", no "in today's fast-paced world".
- **First person, humble and confident.** "One thing I like about myself…" is fine. Bragging is not.
- **The beliefs that recur:**
  - humans stay in control ("It stops before send")
  - learning by doing
  - building in public
  - structure before tools
- **Length:** usually 120 to 300 words. Personal stories can run longer.
- **No hashtags** (or 3 at most). Emoji rarely, once at most, for warmth at the end. 😄
- **Links go in the first comment,** never in the post.
- **Off-limits:** former employer names and branding, confidential work details, salary, and job-search negativity.

## Before publishing, check
- [ ] Does line 1 stand on its own if it's the only thing someone reads?
- [ ] Is there at least one concrete detail (a number, an object, a date)?
- [ ] Is there a contrast line near the end?
- [ ] Does the question have a short, specific answer?
- [ ] Is anything overstated? Is the prototype or real status clear?
- [ ] Nothing from the off-limits list?
