## What it means for Kimchi

1. **Kilo's LinkedIn is developer-first.** Free models, product features and community events. Engagement is low (mostly under 50 reactions). Its enterprise push runs through paid ads and the Cursor switch offer, not its organic feed.
2. **Kilo gives away the same kind of models Kimchi routes to.** MiniMax and NVIDIA Nemotron are offered "free for a limited time". That's a price war on Kimchi's default models, and another reason not to lead with price.
3. **Kilo is growing in Europe.** Its Head of Growth is in Amsterdam ("Growing Europe @ Anaconda"), and it shows up at events in Prague, Paris and Amsterdam.
4. **Its governance means model and provider rules,** which models each team may use. Its spend feature reports spend after the fact ("How much have I spent this month?"). No post says limits are enforced before the work runs, so the gap still holds.
5. **It's small and owned by Anaconda:** a 15-person team with a large community. A buyer comparing vendors will see the free-for-a-year switch offer.
