# Kimchi: the bet and the test

24 Oct 2026. Markdown copy of the Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/6kCLK5VMEGEEMp44kf78UR, made for audit. The overview diagram is described in words.

The go-to-market plan so far for Kimchi (Cast AI's governed AI coding platform): who to target first, what to say, and how to test it in 5 weeks for about $5k. It uses public sources only, plus a first-hand product walkthrough.

## At a glance

The diagram shows 3 stages:
1. **Step 0: validate.** 2 weeks, no spend yet. Talk to sales and the UK BDR, have product confirm the claims, and check Cast AI customers, free-tier sign-ups and 5–8 discovery calls.
2. **Test: 4 weeks, about $5k.** Four tracks:
   - Track 0: Cast AI customers in finance
   - Track 1: 7 hiring-now accounts, 1:1
   - Track 2: look-alikes, ads A1–A3
   - Track 3: tech comparison, control angle
3. **Decide.** Finance and a message both win → scale. No clear message → change the offer. Tech wins → rethink the segment. Everything below the kill line → stop the ads and learn why.

A loop runs back from Decide to Test: at the scale stage, the job-ad search runs monthly and new Tier 1 accounts flow in.

**Who:** UK/EU finance firms with up to 5,000 staff that are hiring an AI platform team.
**What we say:** *Cap the spend. See what every merged PR costs.*
**How we check:** 2 weeks of validation, a 4-week test, then a decision by rules set before launch.

## 1. The bet

### Ideal customer profile (locked)

| Part | Choice | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Industry | Financial services: banks, insurers, fintechs, exchanges, asset managers | 29–30% of AI-rollout hiring in two runs, second only to tech. Of the two, the only one with strict data and vendor rules (DORA, FCA/PRA). |
| Region | UK + EU | 82% of finance AI-rollout jobs (one-month run). Kimchi is hiring in both. |
| Size | Up to 5,000 employees, in 3 LinkedIn tiers: under 500, 501–1,000, 1,001–5,000 | 61% of the 18 finance companies hiring (3-month run). Giant banks build their own or buy direct. |
| Situation | Setting up an AI platform / enablement team to roll out coding agents | 9 of 15 ads read in full name a team. 7 of 11 Tier 1 ads describe a platform builder. |
| Pain | Controlling agent spend and access, and showing what the spend delivers | The strongest theme, across 6 of 8 source types |
| Trigger (new) | An open AI-rollout job ad = hiring now (Tier 1) | The job-ad method itself |

### Excluded, and what would bring each back

- **Individual developers:** they came for credits, not control (Discord: "credits" mentioned 526 times vs "enterprise" 3). Back if free-tier use starts converting to team plans.
- **Finance firms with 5,000+ staff:** they build their own or buy direct (JPMorgan, Goldman, Barclays, Citi). Back once Kimchi has mid-size finance references.
- **Tech:** the same rollout, but looser rules, and spend-tracking tools already compete there. It's the comparison group in the test, and back if it responds as well as finance.
- **Healthcare:** strict rules, but too few jobs (7 companies).
- **Middle East:** no Kimchi signal found.

### Buying committee

| Role | Who | Needs to hear |
|---|---|---|
| Champion | Head of the AI platform / enablement team | Governed agent access live in weeks, without building it |
| Economic buyer | CTO / VP Engineering (finance co-signs) | Capped, predictable spend; cost per team and per merged PR |
| Gatekeeper | Security and compliance | An audit trail, data kept inside, a DORA-ready vendor (Cast AI backing) |
| Users | Developers | They keep the agents they like (Claude Code included) without being blocked |

### Positioning (April Dunford's method)

- **Main alternative:** building it in-house. 7 of 11 Tier 1 ads are hiring someone to build governed AI access by hand.
- **Unique attributes (verified in the product):**
  - budgets per org, team, user and API key
  - cost per merged PR
  - reporting across Claude Code, Kimchi and OpenCode
  - runs in your own cloud (a vendor claim)
- **Category:** governed AI coding platform (Kimchi's own words).
- **Cast AI:** a trust line, not the headline. It becomes the headline only for existing Cast AI customers.

> **For** AI platform leads at UK and EU financial firms rolling out coding agents, **who** need every agent governed, its spend capped and its value visible without spending a quarter building it themselves, **Kimchi is** a governed AI coding platform **that** puts budgets, policy and cost-per-PR reporting over every coding agent your developers use, in your own cloud. **Unlike** building it in-house or bolting on a general AI gateway, Kimchi is ready on day one, built for coding agents, and designed around DORA.

**Primary message:** *Cap the spend. See what every merged PR costs.*

## 2. How we got there

1. **Started from Kimchi's promises:** 7 themes from kimchi.dev.
2. **Looked at the Kimchi Discord (read-only):** people came for credits, and individual use faded. So we target companies, not individuals.
3. **Looked at the wider market:** Reddit, HN, GitHub issues, analysts, regulation. The same complaints keep coming up: costs, visibility, security.
4. **Built the evidence matrix:** a theme counts only if it appears in 3 of the 4 source groups. "Control + proving value" is the strongest, across 6 of 8 source types.
5. **Drew a 2x2** (AI agent use × strictness of rules). Heavy use plus strict rules needs everything Kimchi offers.
6. **Counted job ads:** 4 fixed LinkedIn searches × EU, US and UK, with a fixed keep/drop filter. Finance is 30%, second to tech.
7. **Narrowed it down:** UK + EU is 82% of finance jobs, and the size limit is up to 5,000 employees.
8. **Read the full job ads:** Raisin asks for "routing… usage limits… audit logging", and Kraken for "tiered spend controls".
9. **Made it testable:**
   - re-ran the job-ad search over 3 months
   - did a competitor scan
   - read and scored the 11 accounts
   - did a first-hand product check

**Not counted as evidence:** interview content and the author's own experience.

## 6. The test card

**Hypothesis:** AI platform leads at UK/EU finance firms with up to 5,000 staff respond more than tech firms do, and respond most to the control message.

**The test checks 3 things:**
1. Industry: finance vs tech.
2. Message: control vs proving value vs data inside.
3. Timing signal: Tier 1 (hiring now) vs Tier 2 (look-alikes).

Change one variable per comparison.

### Step 0: validate (2 weeks, no spend)

| Who | What we ask |
|---|---|
| Sales and the UK BDR | Open finance deals, objections |
| Product | Do the claims hold? Is a guided pilot possible? Can admins enforce PR reporting and versions centrally? |
| Customer success / Cast AI | Which customers are UK/EU finance firms? |
| Sign-up data | Which free-tier users have finance work emails? |
| 5–8 discovery calls | How they handle agent spend and control today |

### Tracks

| Track | Who | How | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0: warmest | Cast AI finance customers; free-tier users at finance firms | Account manager intro + email | "You already trust Cast AI with your cloud…" (needs internal data) |
| 1: 1:1 ABM | 7 hiring-now accounts, champion + CTO (14 people) | A personal 1-pager built from their job ad; LinkedIn note, email, and ads shown only to them | One angle per account |
| 2: 1:many | Look-alikes: UK/EU finance, up to 5,000 staff, platform titles | LinkedIn ads A1–A3 + lead form + 3-email nurture | One angle per ad set |
| 3: comparison | 6 tech companies hiring now + ad set A4 | Same as the finance tracks | Control |

### Ads

| Ad set | Audience | Angle | Headline |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Finance | Control | Every coding agent. Spend capped. Fully audited. In your cloud. |
| A2 | Finance | Proving value | See what every merged PR costs, across Claude Code and Kimchi. |
| A3 | Finance | Data inside | Coding agents your regulator can live with. Designed around DORA. |
| A4 | Tech | Control | Same as A1 |

Every ad carries the trust line "Kimchi, from Cast AI" and the same offer.

### 1:1 sequence (4 weeks)

| Week | What |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | Ads shown only to the 7 companies |
| 1 | Champion: LinkedIn note, then email, with the build-vs-buy 1-pager made from their job ad |
| 2 | CTO / VP Eng: cost per team, plus a vendor record that stands up under DORA |
| 3 | Champion: a guided pilot or a 20-minute agent-spend review |
| 4 | Last touch for both |

### Hooks and conversion

1. **Get value:** a research report (*Who's hiring to govern AI agents? UK & EU finance, 2026*) or the 1-pager.
2. **Build trust:** a 3-email nurture, plus the Cast AI line.
3. **Convert:** a guided pilot for one team, or a closed roundtable for 8–10 platform leads.
4. **Close:** a demo, then a meeting.
5. **Rule:** when 2 or more people from one company engage, that company moves to 1:1.

### Budget: capped at about $5,000

| Item | Basis | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Ad sets A1–A4 | 4 × about 15,400 impressions (enough to tell a 0.4% CTR from 0.6% at 90% confidence) × about $70 CPM | about $4,300 |
| Ads to the 7 accounts | | about $500 |
| Outreach and assets | PMM time | $0 |

The benchmarks come from agency data (UK B2B CPM $57; finance CTR 0.30–0.55%). Check the audience size in Campaign Manager first.

### Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 2–13 Nov | Step 0 and building the assets |
| 16 Nov – 11 Dec | The test runs |
| 14–18 Dec | Read the results and decide |

### Success and kill rules

| Area | Success | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| Message | One angle reaches CTR ≥ 0.6% and is 25%+ above the next | Every angle is under 0.3% after 5,000 impressions |
| Industry | Finance beats tech by 25%+ | Tech beats finance |
| Accounts | 3+ of the 7 engage; 2+ meetings | 0 replies |
| Signal | Tier 1 responds clearly better than Tier 2 | No difference |

### Decision per outcome

| Outcome | Decision |
|---|---|
| Finance wins and one message wins | Scale: monthly job-ad search, 1:1 for new hiring accounts, more budget |
| Finance wins, but no clear message | Change the offer |
| Tech wins | Test a cost message for tech; finance becomes second priority |
| Everything is below the kill line | Stop the ads and learn why from the conversations |
| Inconclusive | Run 2 more weeks with the top 2 angles. If still unclear, default to control. |

**When the ICP counts as validated:** finance beats tech on the same message, one message clearly wins, and at least 3 of the 7 hiring-now finance accounts engage. All of this is set before launch.

## 7. Channel choices

| Job | Channels |
|---|---|
| Test the bet | LinkedIn ads with lead forms, 1:1 outreach, email |
| Build trust | GitHub health, the Cast AI name, security pages |
| Build awareness | X, platform engineering communities, KubeCon, the blog, events (at the scale stage) |

- **Developer channels:** developers are users, not buyers. We use them only as a signal.
- **GitHub:** a trust job. A bank's reviewer will read the security issues (for example #1242).
- **X:** not evaluated in depth. It's for awareness, and its targeting is weak.
- **Blog and success stories:** there are no case studies yet. The test creates the first one, and the research report is the blog post.
- **Upselling existing customers:** that's Track 0, which needs internal data.
- **Lead gen:** used, through LinkedIn lead forms. We measure accounts, not leads.
- **Email:** used in 3 places: 1:1, nurture, and warm signals.
- **Webinar:** replaced with a closed roundtable.
- **Self-serve trial:** replaced with a guided pilot, because the free tier needs a paid key.
