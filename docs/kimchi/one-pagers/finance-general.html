02Your regulator's questions

EU firms: under DORA, an AI coding vendor goes into your ICT third-party register, and may need audit rights and an exit plan, depending on what it supports. UK firms: FCA and PRA outsourcing rules raise similar questions. Bring your vendor checklist and we'll mark each line covered, partial or open.
