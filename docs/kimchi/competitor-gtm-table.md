## What this tells us

1. **Each owns one idea:** Devin owns *outcome*, Kilo owns *freedom*, and Kimchi's best claim to own is *control*. Today Kimchi blurs it with cost.
2. **Devin wins on proof:** named banks, outcome numbers and an analyst position. Kimchi has none of these, which is its biggest gap with enterprise buyers.
3. **Kilo wins on reach:** bigger community, faster content, and an aggressive switching offer. Kimchi can't win a bottom-up fight with Kilo.
4. **Only Kimchi sits over other tools and runs in your own cluster.** That's the space neither rival is in.
5. **Kimchi's channels lean on Kubernetes and MLOps events,** which is Cast AI's world. That points again at Cast AI's customers as the first audience.
