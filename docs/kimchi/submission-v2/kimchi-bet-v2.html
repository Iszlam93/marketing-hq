Positioning statement

For platform and engineering leaders at UK and EU financial firms taking coding agents from a few developers to the whole team, Kimchi is the AI platform where agents work on their own and the company sets the limits first: budgets and model limits per team, developer and agent, with a hard stop and the cost of every merged PR. Unlike Claude Code or Cursor, which limit only their own tool, or DX and Jellyfish, which report spend afterwards, Kimchi decides it in advance, with Claude included.

What the seller says

You're hiring someone to build governed access to coding agents. Kimchi gives them limits, budgets and cost per PR on day one, so they start on rollout, not plumbing. I'll show you line by line what's covered.
