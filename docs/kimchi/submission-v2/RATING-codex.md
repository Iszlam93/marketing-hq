# Panel assessment: Senior Product Marketing Manager, EMEA

Scores are out of 5. I treated the two pages as the submission and the appendix as supporting material; the current wording in `kimchi-bet-web.html` governs where the export differs.

## A. Submission

| Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Brief fit | 4 | It makes a specific bet, gives usable copy, states cuts, and supplies a budgeted test; the required actual time spent is still blank. |

## B. Presentation script

| Criterion | Score | Reason |

## C. Overall

**Hire signal: lean yes.** I would advance this candidate: the work shows the ability to make a choice, write a usable message, expose assumptions, and design a bounded learning plan. I would need the discussion to establish sharper statistical and product judgment, especially whether the finance segment and “limits first” promise survive buyer and engineering scrutiny.

## E. Five script changes with the highest payoff

1. **Change:** “Before the bet, three things I'd change in how Kimchi goes to market today.”  
   **To:** “My bet is mid-size UK and EU finance, led by limits set before agents run; I’ll show the evidence and the test, then take questions on where it could fail.”

2. **Change:** “That's the moment someone owns the problem and has a budget, and it's visible on LinkedIn.”  
   **To:** “That posting gives us a named owner to approach; I still need to establish whether that person has budget and authority to buy.”

3. **Change:** “Across six kinds of sources, control is the only need that appears in all six.”  
   **To:** “Control appears across the sources I reviewed, but these are selected public signals, not a representative buyer survey.”

4. **Change:** “None leads with team limits set before the work runs. That's the space Kimchi can own.”  
   **To:** “In the competitor ads I checked, I did not see team limits set before work runs as the lead message; I’d test whether buyers care enough to choose it.”

5. **Change:** “A message wins if it's about 21 clicks ahead of the next at 15,400 impressions each, which is about 90% sure for those two, and I confirm it with calls.”  
   **To:** “I’ll compare the headlines only if delivery is balanced; clicks rank them provisionally, and qualified calls decide whether the message and segment merit a larger test.”
