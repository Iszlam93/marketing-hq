## The gap no one fills (ad library and competitor scan)

Every competitor talks about AI spend, but they **report it after it happens**:
- DX: "AI spend per developer went from $63 to $1,314."
- Jellyfish: "Is your AI spend paying off?"
- Cognition: "What happens to AI costs when usage doubles?"

The ad-library check found **nobody saying "budgets enforced before the spend happens."** Kimchi's budgets are a hard stop: requests fail at the limit (docs). That is control *before*, not reporting *after*.

## Conclusion

Control is the only value that every source group shows, buyers above all (11 of 15 job ads name governance; 5 name spend limits). The open space inside it is **enforcement before the work runs**, where rivals only measure afterwards.
