## Notes
- Claim 1: the PYMNTS wording is "half of its developers... most of them... by the end of 2027". Anthropic's own wording for 2027 is "a majority of software engineers".
- Claim 2: the Risk.net page is paywalled. The "too effective" wording is from its visible opening. No Waters Technology article found. Attribute to Risk.net.
- Claim 3: do not repeat "40,000 developers using Devin". A safe line is: "Citi began rolling out Devin to its developers in July 2025 (it has about 40,000)". A later search snippet mentioned about 14,000 engineers on Devin; not verified.
- Claim 4: Goldman's roughly 12,000 developers is also in the CNBC and American Banker pieces. The bank framed it as a pilot.
- Claim 5: the BofA primary source for 19,000 was not found. The 19,000 attributes to the bank's reporting "this week" per American Banker (Jul 2026).
- Primary-source gap: only claim 1 has a direct company page that was opened.
