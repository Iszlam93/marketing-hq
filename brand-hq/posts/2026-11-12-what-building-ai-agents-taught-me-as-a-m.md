## Post
I didn't learn to code agents.

I learned to design the work they do.

A month of building AI agents for marketing, as a marketer, not an engineer. Four lessons:

→ Structure beats model. The same AI gives better work inside a clear process.
→ Agents need a source of truth. Without approved positioning, every agent invents its own.
→ Boundaries are a design choice. Decide what each agent can read, change and send.
→ Build in public. Every post got me feedback I couldn't have got alone.

None of this is new to product marketers.

It's positioning, process and governance, just with a faster team.

The skills that make a good PMM turn out to be the skills that make agents useful.

What would you build first with an AI team?
