## Why
A craft post built from a real mistake in your own build, not a generic lesson. It shows the PMM judgment hiring managers look for.

## Post
My first AI marketing team started with campaigns. That was the mistake.

The orchestrator took a goal, picked a campaign type and handed out tasks. It produced a lot of material, quickly. Within a day I noticed every piece described the product slightly differently.

Nothing was broken. The agents were doing what any new team does without a brief: filling the gaps with their own assumptions.

So I rebuilt it in the order product marketing actually works. Positioning, ICP, the buying committee and the competitive landscape first. Then the material Sales uses. Then launches and campaigns on top.

It's the advice I'd give a new marketing hire, and it took building it with agents for me to see how often we skip it with people too. We start with the campaign because it's visible. The foundation work stays invisible until it's missing.

If you're adding AI to your marketing, write the positioning down before you write a single prompt. The agents will use it. So will your colleagues.
