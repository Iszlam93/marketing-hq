## Why
Keeps Building with AI in public near its ~40% share over the four weeks to 1 Dec, and adds the "what broke" side of building in public, which the series hasn't shown yet. Honest failures make the wins more believable.

Real events from the repo: on 20 Oct your "send back" comment from the app was saved twice and partly landed under the Revision note instead of Feedback, so the agent reading feedback could miss it (fixed 23 Oct). And on 29 Oct Marketing HQ marked a second "Summer campaign" request as a duplicate instead of running it twice. The lesson for marketers: the handoffs between people and agents break before the agents do.

When it's drafted (Mon 30 Nov), the planner will ask you how you noticed the misplaced feedback and which of the two fixes taught you more.
