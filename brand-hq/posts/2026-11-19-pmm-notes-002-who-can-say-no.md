## Why
Product marketing craft is under its ~35% share this month (only the 5 Nov post), and AI for marketers is over. This one is about the buying committee: one message for the champion, a different one for the economic buyer and the people who can block the deal. It's a topic the roles you're in process for keep raising (ICP, persona messaging, champion vs economic buyer), and it hasn't come up in the last 60 days.

Starting material: Marketing HQ's Foundations has its own "Buying committee" tab next to ICP and personas, because one message for everyone didn't work. When it's drafted (Mon 16 Nov), the planner will ask you for one real moment from your B2B work where someone outside the champion's view slowed or stopped a deal (no company names).
