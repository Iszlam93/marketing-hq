## Post
I built an AI team to help run my LinkedIn.

Its first suggestion was to stop posting so much about AI.

The team is small: a Brand Manager that owns the goal, a Planner that always has the next 30 days mapped, a Writer, a Designer and a Reminder.

Before writing anything, it read my posts to learn how I actually write. Short lines. Concrete details. A question at the end.

Then the Planner looked at my last three posts. All three were about building with AI.

Its recommendation: more product marketing craft. Because that's what the people I want to reach actually hire for.

It was right.

Here's the part I care about most: it plans, drafts and designs, but it stops before post. Every word still goes through me.

Automation takes the busywork.
Judgment stays with me.

It's an early prototype, and I'm testing it on myself first.

Would you let an agent plan your content, if you still approved every post?
