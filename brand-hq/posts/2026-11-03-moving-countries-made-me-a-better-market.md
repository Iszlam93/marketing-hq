Moving countries made me a better marketer.

Not because of a new job title.

Because almost nothing I assumed was true anymore.

When I moved from Egypt to the Netherlands, I had to relearn small things: how people say no, how fast decisions happen, what "direct" really means.

**[add: one concrete moment, e.g. a meeting, an email or a phrase that surprised you]**

It taught me three things I still use every day:

→ My context isn't the customer's context. Check, don't assume.
→ Simple language travels further than clever language.
→ Starting again is a skill. You get better at it each time.

Good marketing starts with knowing your audience.

Living abroad is a crash course in learning one from scratch.
