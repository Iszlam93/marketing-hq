# Research 2a: Your customers

**The question:** who already buys from us, who stays, and who do we lose to?

Your own customers are the strongest evidence you have: real people already chose to pay. Start here whenever there is any customer data. A company with no customers yet writes "no customer data: the decision rests on public evidence" and moves on to 2b.

## What you need
One of these, from the best available source:
- **Best:** won and lost deals from the CRM for the last 12 to 24 months, with industry, company size, region, buyer role, deal value, days to close, and loss reason.
- **Good:** a list of current customers with industry, size and how long they have stayed.
- **Minimum:** the founder's or sales lead's list of the 10 best and 5 worst customers, and why.

With a **CRM connector**, pull the deal list yourself (read only, no edits). Without one, ask the user once for an export or the short list.

## Steps
1. **Write down the groups before you look:** for example by industry, size band, region and buyer role. Use 3 to 5 size bands that match how the company sells.
2. **Count each group.** For each: number of deals, number won, win rate (won out of total), average deal value, typical days to close. If you have it, also how many customers stayed after a year.
3. **Look for the surprise.** Which group wins more, closes faster or stays longer than you expected? Which group takes a lot of sales time but rarely buys?
4. **Read the loss reasons.** Count them: price, missing feature, chose a competitor (which), no decision, wrong fit. "No decision" often means the problem was not urgent for that group.
5. **Check the numbers are big enough.** A group with fewer than about 10 deals is a **hint**, not proof. Tag it **Assumes** and say what more data would settle it.
6. **Write the pattern** in 2 or 3 sentences: who buys best, who to avoid, and what the losses say.

## What to write in the evidence log
```
Customer pattern (source: <CRM export / customer list / founder list>, <date>)
| Group | Deals | Won | Win rate | Avg value | Days to close | Stayed 1 yr | Shows / Assumes |
Loss reasons: <reason: count of total>
Surprise: <one line>
Pattern: <2 to 3 sentences>
```

## Checks
- [ ] Groups were chosen before looking at results.
- [ ] Every win rate shows "won out of total".
- [ ] Groups under about 10 deals are tagged Assumes.
- [ ] No customer or contact names appear in the shared page; use groups only.
- [ ] Nothing was changed in the CRM.

## Example (fictional)
A field-service scheduling tool has 70 deals over 18 months. Heating and plumbing firms: 30 deals, 14 won (47%), 21 days to close. All other trades: 40 deals, 8 won (20%), 38 days to close. Top loss reason outside heating and plumbing: "no decision" (15 of 32 losses). Pattern: heating and plumbing firms buy faster and more often; other trades rarely feel the problem is urgent.
