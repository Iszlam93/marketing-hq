# The one-page output

Fill this at the end. Page 1 is the decision; page 2 is the test. Everything else (the full evidence log, research notes) goes in an appendix.

```
# Where to start: <product> in <market>

## The answer in three sentences
<Who to go after first, and the trigger. The buyer and the message. How sure I am, and the weakest link.>

## The question
- Asked by / for: <who, by when>
- Goal: <measurable, dated>
- Limits: <time, budget, region, sources>
- Not evidence: <...>
- Tools used: <connectors, or "web search only">; customer data: <yes / no>

## The facts (summary; full log in the appendix)
- Our customers: <pattern, or "no customer data">
- Buying signals: <funnel A → B → C → D with % and "out of">
- Buyer voice: <loudest pain, N of M threads; words to reuse>
- Competitors: <crowded / owned / open claim>
- Against my choice: <what I looked for, what I found>
- Missing: <what I could not check>

## The choice
| Group | Problem now | Reason to act | Can pay | Can reach | Can win today | Total | Proof |
Chosen: <group>, because <deciding question>. Runner-up: <group>, out because <reason>.
Buyer: <role, owns, asked to>   Trigger: <sentence>
Message: <headline> · <proof line> · <why now>   Do not say: <...>
Left out: <who, why>   Near-miss: <who, why it lost>
How sure: group <H/M/L, why> · buyer <...> · message <...>   Weakest link: <...>

## The test
Hypothesis: <...>
Design and size: <versions, control, teaching control, n per version, cost, end date>
Rules: <winner rule, floor, one extension>
| Result | What I saw | What I do next |   (Works / Fails / Unclear / Broken)
Business result (reported, not caused): <...>
Owners: <...>

## Hard questions
1. <Q> → <A, with fact #>
2. <Q> → <A>
3. <Q> → <A>
If it fails, the most likely reason: <...>

## Gaps
- [MISSING: ...]

## Sources
- <link or connector + filters, date>
```

## Final checks
- [ ] Goal is measurable and dated; limits are met.
- [ ] "Not evidence", searches, scoring and the winner rule were written before the results.
- [ ] Every fact is tagged Shows or Assumes, with a source and date; every number has "out of how many".
- [ ] At least three groups were scored, each score with proof; exactly one was chosen.
- [ ] The message uses only what the product does today; the "do not say" list is respected.
- [ ] Who is left out, the near-miss and how sure I am (per part) are written.
- [ ] The test targets the weakest link and has all four result rows.
- [ ] No personal data in the page; nothing was sent, posted or changed in any tool.
