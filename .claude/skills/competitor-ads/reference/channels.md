# The ad libraries

All free and public. Coverage changes, so trust what the library shows today. Checked: November 2026.

| Platform | Link | Covers | What you can see | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | linkedin.com/ad-library | LinkedIn ads, worldwide | ad text and creative, advertiser, who paid, dates; search by company, payer or keyword | ads since June 2023, kept about a year; views and targeting only for ads shown in the EU |
| **Google** | adstransparency.google.com | Search, YouTube, Display, Shopping, Maps, Play | ads by advertiser or website, by region, date and format | verified advertisers worldwide, all advertisers in Europe; short history |
| **Meta** | facebook.com/ads/library | Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network, worldwide | ad text and creative, start date, platforms, versions; set the country | active ads only (longer history for ads shown in the EU and UK) |
| **TikTok** | library.tiktok.com | TikTok ads shown in Europe and the UK | creative, advertiser, dates, audience-size bands | Europe and UK only; since October 2022 |
| **Microsoft (Bing)** | search "Microsoft Ad Library" | Bing search ads in Europe | search by advertiser or ad words | Europe only |
| **X** | search "X Ads Transparency" | X ads shown in Europe | advertiser, who paid, targeting, views; search by account, country, dates | Europe only |
| **Pinterest** | search "Pinterest Ads Repository" | Pinterest ads in Europe | about 12 months of ads | Europe only |
| **Snapchat** | search "Snapchat Ads Gallery" | Snapchat ads in Europe | basic ad details | Europe only |

## How to search each one
- **LinkedIn:** search the company name; check the advertiser matches the company page.
- **Google:** search the website domain (for example hubspot.com) rather than the name; set the region.
- **Meta:** set the country and "All ads", then search the company name and pick its Page from the list (not a keyword search).
- **TikTok:** set a European country, then search the advertiser name.

## Access, tested November 2026
- **The user's own browser, including Claude in Chrome:** Meta and LinkedIn open and load. Google and TikTok not yet tested this way.
- **Automated visitors from a server:** blocked or empty on all four. Do not rely on a plain web reader.
