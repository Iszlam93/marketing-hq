# The ad libraries

All of these are free and public. Coverage changes often, so check the library itself before relying on a detail. Checked: November 2026.

## The main four

| Library | Link | Covers | What you can see | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta Ad Library** | facebook.com/ads/library | Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network; active ads worldwide | ad text and creative, start date, platforms, versions; set the country | Live ads only, except political ads and ads shown in the EU and UK, which are kept longer. No spend for normal ads. |
| **Google Ads Transparency Center** | adstransparency.google.com | Search, YouTube, Display, Shopping, Maps, Play | ads by advertiser or domain, by region, date range and format | Verified advertisers worldwide; all advertisers in Europe. Thin records; sources disagree on how long ads are kept (about 30 days to 13 months). No spend. |
| **LinkedIn Ad Library** | linkedin.com/ad-library | LinkedIn sponsored content, worldwide | ad text and creative, advertiser, who paid, dates; search by company, payer or keyword | Ads since June 2023, kept about a year after the last view. Views and top targeting shown only for ads aimed at the EU. No spend. |
| **TikTok Commercial Content Library** | library.tiktok.com | TikTok ads shown in the EU, EEA, Switzerland and UK | ad creative, advertiser, dates, audience-size bands | Europe only. Since October 2022. The EU found its search incomplete in 2025; TikTok has committed to improve it. No spend. |

## Others, mostly Europe only
Search for these by name; they mainly exist because of EU rules, so they show ads shown in Europe.

| Library | Covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Microsoft Ad Library** | Bing search ads in the EU/EEA | search by advertiser or words in the ad; scope outside Europe unclear |
| **X Ads Transparency** | ads on X shown in the EU | advertiser, who paid, main targeting, views; search by account, country and dates |
| **Pinterest Ads Repository** | Pinterest ads in the EU | about 12 months of records |
| **Snapchat Ads Gallery** | Snapchat ads in the EU | more limited than Meta and Google |

## Not available
- **Reddit:** no public library of competitors' ads. Reddit's "Ads Inspiration Library" shows hand-picked top ads, not a given competitor's ads.
- **Amazon:** no confirmed public library for competitors' ads.
- **Spend:** no library shows spend for normal commercial ads. Use ad counts, start dates and the number of versions as the signal of effort.

## Access, tested November 2026
- **Your own browser, including Claude in Chrome:** Meta and LinkedIn open and load. Google and TikTok not yet tested this way.
- **Automated visitors from a server:** Meta and LinkedIn refused (blocked); Google and TikTok returned an empty page or a sign-in frame with no ads. Do not rely on Claude's plain web reader for ad libraries.
