# Battlecard: Demo Product vs main competitor

**Use when:** a finance buyer has a close deadline and is also talking to the main competitor.

## Lead with
1. **Time to value.** Cuts month-end close by 2 days on average (customer survey, Sep 2026).
2. **No integration project.** It sits next to the ERP; setup takes days, not months.

## Handle the objection
> "Our auditors already know them."

Say: "That makes sense. Ask your auditor what they need to see. Our exception log gives them a full trail of every match."

## Don't say
- "Fully automated." A person still reviews exceptions.

## Proof points
- 80% of invoices matched without manual work in month one (onboarding report, Q3 2026)
