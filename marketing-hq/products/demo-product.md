## Description
A B2B SaaS tool that helps finance teams at mid-size companies close the month faster. It matches invoices to purchase orders and flags exceptions for a person to check.

## Audience
Buyer: finance leaders (CFO, finance director) at companies with 200 to 2,000 staff. Users: accounts payable and controlling teams.

## Approved claims
- Cuts month-end close by 2 days on average — source: demo customer survey, Sep 2026, 41 answers
- Matches 80% of invoices without manual work in the first month — source: demo onboarding report, Q3 2026

## Claims to avoid
- Fully automated (a person still reviews exceptions)
- Replaces the ERP (it sits next to it)
